# BRP manual notes (pp1-16)
- Midrail 2001+ chassis. Coil-over front, rear: torsion bar / coil-over (non short link) / short link variants.
- LS% 54.2-54.4 rec (2200lb before driver). Rear% BB 63.5-64.5 (setup proc says 64-64.5). Diagonal 46.5-47.5 start. LR wt = LR-RR.
- More rear% = tighter on throttle; too much = pendulum loose entry. More LR wt = tighter on throttle, loose entry. Wheel lead: longer LS WB = tighter. Lower left side = tighter exit, less sidebite (loose mid).
- Rake: higher front = tighter entry; higher rear = looser exit. Higher ride heights = more roll.
- Caster: RF 11.5, LF 7.5 (built into axle; split 3-4deg). More caster: stiffer steering, less LR on entry (loose), more LR on exit (tight).
- Camber: built into axle; adjustable spindles +/-2deg. RF -8, LF +1.5..+2. Toe out 1/8-3/16 at tires.
- Wheels: LF 10x4 off, RF 12x5, LR 13x3, RR 14x3 (offset/backset affects %s).
- Frame heights start: LF/RF 5 1/4 (5 1/2 AR tires), LR/RR 6 1/2.
- Brake: 1" front MC top of balance bar, 7/8 rear bottom.
- Stagger: front 1/2-1 1/2 (smaller LF). Rear 7-8 heavy, 6-7 medium, 5-6 slick. More = looser entry/apex; too little = slow entry, push on throttle; too much = slower straights, loose exit. Err high.
- Generic specs: Springs LF 225, RF 150-175 (BB 175), LR 100-200 coil, RR 165-200. Torsion LR 950, RR 925 rollers rear holes (front holes softer). Shocks Integra RSR-76 all. Tire psi Hoosier LF9 RF9 LR6 RR10; American Racer 10/10/8/12.
- Rear springs coil-over: start 200#, slick common pair 175. Short link LR 150 + 75 tender, RR 175.
- Banked/heavy track: stiffer RR bar (950). RR helper 80# coil, nut touching at ride height. Stiffer RR shock/more gas.
- Wheelbase: square rear 9" (-5 birdcage timing) or 8 3/4 (0 timing); coil/shortlink must be 9". Front 1 1/2" axle-to-shock-tower. WB 105.5-105.75. Tight: lead LR ahead 3/8 (also softens LR rate).
- LR weight: dry slick 60-110 (start 40); wet/tacky 0-30 (start 30); very heavy 30# RR weight.
- Radius rods (no heims): LF/RF 25 (opt 24.5 LF top), front panhard 21, LR long 27.5, RR long 28, RS rear panhard 15, LR short 17.5, RR short 18.5, LS rear panhard 12.
- Rear spring rod: compress spring 3.75 tacky / 4 normal / 4.25 slick.
- Torque arm 0deg BRP426 or -3deg BRP9743 (rec). Rubber biscuit.
- Rear panhard RS start: 2nd hole down on rear end, 6th hole down outside on BRP4550 plates (non adj) or ~3" up on adjustable outboard mount. LS panhard: top hole on rear end, 3rd hole up on outside plates. Cockpit adjustable mounts exist.
- Coil nut 8TPI: 1 turn = 1/8" ~6lb. Torsion stop bolt 1 turn = 1/4" ~12lb. LR bar preload 5-6 turns, RR 6-7 (no shocks).
- LS% set via front panhard (shorten till LF shock bolt 1/4" off tower) then rear panhard. Weights diagonal. LR weight change: spread over 4 corners.
# pp17-25
- Torsion preload: LR 5-6 turns, RR 6-7. Stop on RH side controls LR bar.
- Front panhard slider: start at bottom (older) / 2nd hole from bottom (newer welded brackets). Drop bracket on axle: 2nd hole down from top (BRP1503 2 holes off axle CL; BRP1622 1 hole). Center front end; 21" panhard near bottomed.
- Caster set on blocks +1.5 over target (13/9 on blocks -> 11.5/7.5 at ride ht). Adjusted with top or bottom radius rods (turns). Front WB: axle 1.5" from shock towers. RF top radius rod must be free (bind!).
- Rear (torsion): outer panhard 3" on adjustable / 6th hole from top on non-adj; inner 2nd hole from top. Center rear end 11 7/16 each side (can offset for LS%). Square 8 3/4 or 9. Rear radius rods start middle holes. Spring rod 3 3/4 heavy. Rollers bottom rear holes.
- Birdcage timing: Option A +2deg fwd w/ -3 torque arm (+5 rel cover): helps entry, looser, for limited HP / tracks needing loose. Option B -3deg behind (0 rel cover): tighter entry & accel, high HP & dry tracks. 8-bolt ~2.75.
- Torque arm rubbers: shipped length heavy; cut down to min 1 1/8" for dry slick. Triple biscuit BRP9425 = more neg pinion, more hook up time. Torque arm lengths 28 1/8, 29 5/8, 31 1/8, 32 5/8. Slider heim 2 1/4" (pinion angle).

- Balance bar: pushrod clevis 3 3/4 front, 4 rear start; bias adjust via balance bar (turns).
# pp35-43
- Brake pressures: all-front 600F/400R; 50/50 450/550; all-rear 320/700.
- Throttle linkage: cross rod hole choice (closer to pivot = slower/more control). Slow throttle for slick. Discrete holes.
- Bind list (good for diagnostic scenarios): seized torsion bearings/rollers, bent bars, bent rod ends, torque arm slider dry, bent rear tubes, bent shock shafts, dented shock bodies, rear end hitting seat plate, shocks bottoming, driveshaft phasing/end play 1/2-3/4, torsion arms rubbing birdcages/radius rods, drag link rubbing, shock bolt hitting chassis. Bound front radius rod -> driver says tight entry (misdiagnosis!).
- Rear radius rods: both middle = stock, reverse roll steer, driveable push. Raise RR only: more roll steer, looser entry & on throttle. Raise LR: looser entry, better drive on throttle. Raise both: max roll steer, loose, erratic on throttle, more drive.
- 29" torsion bar rates table (dia x arm length 14-18"): .875:147/128/112/100/89; .900:164/143/126/111/99; .925:183/160/140/124/111; .950:204/178/156/138/123; .975:226/197/173/153/137; 1.000:250/218/192/170/152; 1.025:276/241/212/187/167; 1.050:304/265/233/206/184; 1.075:334/291/256/[277 typo ~227]/202; 1.100:367/319/281/249/222; 1.125:401/349/307/272/243.
- TIGHT ON ENTRY fixes: free front bind; smaller spoiler/gurney; +rear stagger; less RR offset (wheel out); more RR weight (0-30 LR start); more rear brake; caster split 4deg; more RF camber; stiffer LF spring (RF shock 5" shaft); lead LR 3/8; LR shock more gas/comp/rebound; LF shock more rebound; front panhard lower outboard; RS rear panhard raise outboard/lower inboard; LS panhard/Jbar lower outboard/raise inboard; stiffen LR or both rear; raise rear frame ht or lower front, flatten L-R (NOTE contradicts rake text p4!); raise RR radius rod; balance LS 54-54.4 rear 64-66.
# pp44-51 (troubleshooting tables, these become the scenario answer keys)
- LOOSE ENTRY: check driver entry speed/brake loose; reduce rear stagger (not too low); stiffen RF/soften LF + RF comp; more front brake/stickier pads; free rear bind; more LR wt (too much can loosen); soften LR rebound/less N2; RS panhard lower outboard/raise inboard; LS panhard/Jbar raise outboard/lower inboard; softer LR bar or roller to front hole; toe out up to 1/4; raise front/lower rear ht; more spoiler/gurney fwd; both rear radius rods bottom holes; sipe/grind tires (start 1/8 deep).
- TIGHT APEX OFF THROTTLE: RS panhard raise both ends 1"; LS panhard/Jbar lower outboard; RR radius rod top hole; 25# ballast high on nose to LF; more RF rebound; RR stiffer comp only or less rebound; less LR rebound; stiffer RR spring; max rear end offset & rear track per rules; raise RR ht 1/2 & lower LF 1/2.
- LOOSE APEX OFF THROTTLE: RS panhard lower outboard; LS raise outboard; softer LR spring/roller front hole; less LR wt when slick (0-20); raise heights 1/4-1/2 or LF/LR/RF only; less LF rebound; less LR rebound; ballast raise over rear end.
- TIGHT APEX ON THROTTLE: remove spring rod->solid; less LR wt; stiffer LR spring (roller back hole); raise rear 1/4-1/2; LR N2 up to 250#; more LF & RF rebound; less RR rebound.
- LOOSE APEX ON THROTTLE: softer LR spring/roller front; stiffer RF (max 175)/softer LF; more RR rebound; less LF rebound; lower LR N2; more LR wt 40-60 or 110; spring rod at 4 1/4; -3 torque arm, pickup 29 1/2 hole; birdcage timing 0.
- TIGHT EXIT: +rear stagger; less LR wt; lead LR 3/8; stiffer LR (roller back); stiffen front rebound; raise rear 1/4 / drop front 1/4; less rear %, more LF; solid rod; RR radius rod top hole, LR bottom.
- LOOSE EXIT: (check tight-entry shearing loose!) less stagger; more LR wt; soften front rebound/digressive; soften LR comp; softer LR (roller front); stiffer RF max 175; raise front/lower rear; square rear or RR fwd / LR back 1/4; spring rod 4 1/4; shorten torque arm (2nd->3rd hole, 29 1/2); sipe/grind; pressure buildup max 4# (air drier); raise LR radius rod.
- FORWARD BITE: usually caused by poor entry/mid speed; soften front rebound; soften rear comp/digressive LR; stiffer RF/softer LF; softer LR (or both); more LR wt & rear%; lower rear/raise front; softer rear tire psi; more spoiler; raise both rear rods (or LR); shorten torque arm; N2 125 front / 75 rear.
- Torque arm pickup holes from transmission: 2nd, 3rd (=29 1/2 from bottom hole in rear end).
# pp52-57
- SIDE BITE: often caused by tight entry -> shears loose. RS panhard lower outboard/raise inboard (or rubber panhard BRP9120); LS panhard/Jbar raise outboard or raise inboard; lower rear tire psi; less LR wt; softer RR spring (too soft rolls too much); RR softer comp/stiffer rebound; raise all 4 corners (raise CG), flatten L-R; raise ballast/over rear end; doors max legal; birdcage timing.
- Shocks: springs control how much weight, shocks how fast. Compression = how fast weight gets to corner, rebound = how long it stays. Shocks don't affect mid-turn balance (steady state!). N2: fronts ~100psi, rears 125-150; change max +/-50; 25psi ~ 10lb spring. Low shaft speed 1-5 ips = cornering; high 6-10 = bumps/cushion. Track slows (18s tacky heat -> 21s slick feature) => car motion slows, shock range shifts lower (2-6 -> 1-4 ips). Linear vs digressive (digressive: more grip, less predictable).
- Shock tips: tune entry first, exit last; loose-in 9/10 alignment not shocks; dry slick: reduce left side rebound, reduce right side compression.
- Baseline packages: Integra heavy (LF STD+1R, RF STD+1C, LR/RR Medium), avg-slick (all Standard), short link (FSR/RSR). Fox heavy LF 60C/200R RF 75/165 LR 115/230 RR 120/270; Fox slick (D=digressive) LF 65/190 RF 75/165 LR 145/145 RR 100/200; Bilstein heavy LF 208/55 RF 208/72 LR 230/120 RR 270/120; slick LR 162/120 RR 230/72.
- Springs: LR extension load built by softer spring (more preload). RR preload e.g. 14x200 on 9" shock compressed to 13. Spring rubbers stiffen.
# Shock tuning chart p53 (column-mapped)
- Entry push: RF bump decrease; LF rebound increase; LR rebound increase
- Mid push: LF rebound increase
- Exit push: RF rebound increase; LR bump increase
- Entry loose: RF bump increase; LR rebound decrease
- Mid loose: LF rebound decrease; RR bump decrease (column position ambiguous); RR rebound increase
- Exit loose: RF rebound decrease; LR bump decrease
